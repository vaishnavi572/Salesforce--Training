# Salesforce Developer Bridge Program – Day 6

This repository contains the Day 6 activities completed as part of the Salesforce Developer Bridge Program.

## Topics Covered

* Apex Triggers
* Event-Driven Automation
* Business Events
* Before and After Triggers
* Trigger Architecture
* Service Classes
* Salesforce Automation Best Practices

## Activities Completed

* Learned how Apex Triggers respond to business events.
* Understood the difference between Trigger and Service responsibilities.
* Explored Before and After Trigger scenarios.
* Designed automation for the Placement Management System.
* Studied Trigger architecture and maintainability principles.
* Practiced Trigger-related interview questions and design discussions.

## Key Learnings

* Triggers should observe events and delegate work to Service Classes.
* Business logic should not be placed directly inside Triggers.
* Automation helps reduce manual effort and improve consistency.
* Good architecture makes applications easier to maintain and extend.

## Project

**Placement Management System**

Implemented event-driven automation concepts to automatically respond to important business events such as application creation, status updates, and placement activities.
